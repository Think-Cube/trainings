## How our trainings work

<div class="grid" markdown>

:material-flask-outline: **Lab first**
: Roughly half of the time is hands-on. Each module ends with a lab that builds on the previous one.

:material-update: **Always current**
: Labs are re-verified before every course run – versions, commands and cloud defaults change fast.

:material-account-group-outline: **For your team**
: Delivered on-site or remote, in English or Polish, tailored to your stack and environments.

:material-source-branch: **Open materials**
: Participants keep the lab repositories – and can repeat every exercise after the course.

</div>
